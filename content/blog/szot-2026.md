### [ENG] 9th Rebel Fencing HEMA Training Camp

The 9th Rebel Fencing HEMA Training Camp took place this August in Jagniątków, Karkonosze.

📅 **15–21 August 2026** · 📍 **Jagniątków, Karkonosze, Poland** · 🏋️ **approx. 40 h of training**
⚔️ **rapier & dagger · smallsword · sabre · longsword**

Seven days of fencing, technique, sparring, mountain air and Rebel camp chaos – open to beginners and advanced fencers alike.
